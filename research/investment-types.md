# Investment Types: Historical Return and Risk Context

There is no dependable fixed annual return for any asset class. The ranges below are rough,
long-term nominal historical annualized returns, typically before personal taxes and often before
fund fees. They are context rather than forecasts.

| Investment type | Typical access | Approx. long-term annual return | Risk factor | Main risks |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| High-interest savings / cash | Bank, savings ETF | 1-4% | 1/5 - Low | Inflation can exceed returns; deposit and issuer limits |
| GICs / term deposits | Bank or broker | 2-5% | 1/5 - Low | Funds may be locked in; inflation and issuer risk |
| Government bonds | Bond ETF or individual bonds | 3-6% | 2/5 - Low-moderate | Interest-rate sensitivity; inflation |
| Investment-grade corporate bonds | Bond ETF or bonds | 4-7% | 2-3/5 - Low-moderate | Credit/default risk plus rate sensitivity |
| Dividend stocks / equity-income funds | ETF, mutual fund, stocks | 7-10% | 3-4/5 - Moderate-high | Stock-market declines; dividend cuts; sector concentration |
| Broad Canadian equities | TSX index fund / ETF | 7-9% | 4/5 - High | Commodity and financial-sector concentration |
| Broad global equities | Global index fund / ETF | 8-10% | 4/5 - High | Market declines, currency, geopolitical risk |
| US large-cap equities | S&P 500 fund / ETF | About 10% in USD | 4/5 - High | Valuation, concentration, CAD-USD currency movement |
| REITs | Public REIT ETF or REIT shares | 7-10% | 3-4/5 - Moderate-high | Interest rates, property cycles, debt, tenant vacancies |
| Direct rental real estate | Property ownership | 5-10%+ total return | 3-5/5 - Moderate-very high | Leverage, vacancies, maintenance, illiquidity, local-market risk |
| Infrastructure | Infrastructure ETF/private fund | 6-10% | 3/5 - Moderate | Regulatory, interest-rate, project, and valuation risk |
| Private credit | Private fund | 7-12% | 3-4/5 - Moderate-high | Illiquidity, borrower defaults, manager risk |
| Private equity | Private fund / limited partnership | 10-15%+ target or historical range | 5/5 - Very high | Illiquidity, high fees, valuation opacity, manager selection |
| Venture capital | Private fund / direct startup investment | Highly variable; many investments lose value | 5/5 - Very high | Very high failure rate, illiquidity, long time horizon |
| Commodities / gold | ETF, futures, physical holdings | 0-6% long-term, highly variable | 3-4/5 - Moderate-high | No cash flow, sharp price swings, inflation/geopolitics |
| Cryptocurrency | Exchange or ETF, where available | No reliable long-term estimate | 5/5 - Very high | Extreme volatility, custody/regulatory/platform risk |

## Notes on Private Equity and REITs

**Private equity** means ownership in non-public companies, normally through a fund that locks up
capital for many years. Its reported returns can look high, but comparisons with public markets
are complicated by high fees, leverage, delayed valuation updates, and the inability to sell
quickly.

**REITs** (real estate investment trusts) own or finance income-producing properties, including
apartments, warehouses, offices, and data centres. Public REIT ETFs are generally more liquid and
diversified than buying rental property directly, but their prices can still move like equities,
particularly when interest rates change.

## Portfolio Context

A diversified portfolio often combines categories rather than seeking the single
highest-returning one: cash or GICs for near-term needs, bonds for stability, broad equities for
long-term growth, and smaller optional allocations to REITs, private markets, or other
alternatives only when their risks and liquidity constraints fit the goal.
